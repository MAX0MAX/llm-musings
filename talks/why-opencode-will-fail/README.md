# Why opencode Will Fail

> A deliberately provocative hypothesis about an open-source coding-agent product.

**Status:** Planned

## About this talk

This is a personal argument, not a verdict. I want to make the word "fail" concrete, identify the assumptions behind my prediction, and examine evidence that could prove me wrong.

The goal is to use opencode as a case study for a broader question: what does it take for an open-source coding agent to become a durable product rather than an impressive tool?

## Questions I want to answer

- What would success or failure mean for opencode?
- Which parts of its technical and product strategy are genuinely strong?
- Where might the project struggle with differentiation or distribution?
- How important are model access, integrations, trust, and user experience?
- What evidence would cause me to change my mind?

## Draft outline

1. The thesis and what I mean by "fail"
2. Why opencode is compelling today
3. The coding-agent market around it
4. Possible technical and product failure modes
5. The strongest counterarguments
6. Signals to watch and conditions for changing my view
